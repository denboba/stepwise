# Stepwise experience

Follow #[[file:docs/UX_DESIGN.md]] and #[[file:docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md]]. Make the main workspace a helpful conversation with a clear next action and expandable explanations; provide a living graph and project memory as secondary but discoverable surfaces. Support "How?", "Why?", "I'm stuck", "That failed", "Show graph", "Pause" and "What changed?" without losing context.

Design should be elegant, responsive, keyboard-accessible and honest about evidence and uncertainty. No fabricated research or automatic completion.
