# Stepwise product context

Stepwise is an outcome-oriented AI expert companion: it understands a person's goal, asks useful questions, offers the next safe action, remembers evidence and attempts, diagnoses failure, replans, and verifies results. It is **not** a generic chatbot or rigid wizard. The product vision is not yet fully implemented.

**North star:** Plan globally. Guide contextually. Remember continuously. Adapt intelligently. Verify outcomes.

Read #[[file:PRODUCT.md]], #[[file:docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md]], and #[[file:roadmap.md]] for business rules, acceptance criteria and sequencing. Do not call the app production-ready without verified end-to-end results. Expert guidance never grants professional credentials or permission for hazardous tasks.
